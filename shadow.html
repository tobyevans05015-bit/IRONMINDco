The Work — Step Three

Release

Not a passive read — an actual workbook. Each step is its own small entry, one at a time, working through a single pattern you've already identified.

What Release Actually Means

Release doesn't mean the pattern vanishes the moment you name it. It means the belief underneath it stops running unexamined — you've felt where it lives, said what it cost, and consciously chosen what replaces it. Real shadow work isn't linear: a pattern can resurface even after you've worked through it here, and that's normal, not failure. You can run this workbook again for the same pattern any time it comes back around.

The Goal: Feel It Until It Moves

Emotion is energy that wants to move through the body and complete itself. Most patterns form because a feeling got interrupted — pushed down, distracted from, or never allowed to finish. The goal below isn't to think your way past it. It's to actually feel it fully enough that it breaks up and leaves, instead of going back underground.

  1. Locate it. Notice where in your body this emotion actually sits right now — chest, jaw, gut, shoulders. Be specific.
  2. Breathe into it, don't push it away. Send your next few breaths directly into that spot instead of distracting yourself from it.
  3. Let it move. It may intensify, shake, tighten, or want to come out as a sigh, a sound, or tears. Let it, without judging it or acting on it destructively.
  4. Stay with it until it peaks and drops. Keep breathing and observing. There's usually a peak of intensity, then a noticeable release — that drop is the emotion actually discharging, not being suppressed again.
  5. Notice what's left. When it eases, check what's underneath — often it's calm, relief, or a quieter, more honest version of the same feeling. That's the sign it's actually moved, not just been argued with.
