Funnel Stage

04

Bodywork

Format

Clone format is nearly overplayed. Series are back and convert views to followers better than anything else. Green screen is the easiest entry point if you're short on setup.

05

Ignition

Hook

Simple and slightly aggressive beats clever. Your two proven hook mechanisms: self-diagnostic framing ("do you come under any of these") and a broad accusation. Whatever you write, plan to reopen the loop again roughly every 15 seconds — one hook does not carry a whole video.

06

Under the Hood

Stance & Diagnosis

The stance is the unhedged position you actually hold — say it once, flat, no softening. The diagnosis is the real content underneath it, the thing that's actually teaching someone something.

07

Fuel Mix

Illustration

This is what makes the diagnosis land instead of just being information — story, case, metaphor, stat, or quote.

08

Traction

Conversation Driver & Polarization

Pick what this video is actually optimizing for — comments, sends, or saves rarely all peak from the same video. If you're taking a stance, know exactly who it's for and who it pushes away.

If the honest answer is no — reconsider it. Manufactured outrage builds views and zero fandom.
